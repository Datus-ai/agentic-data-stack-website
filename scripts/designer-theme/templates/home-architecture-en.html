WHAT IS AGENTIC DATA STACK?

When agents become users—and builders—of data systems.

The Agentic Data Stack is a data technology stack designed and evolved for AI agents, enabling them to understand data and business context and carry out discovery, development, analysis, and operations under governed, verifiable conditions.

The Modern Data Stack was built around how people work. The agentic era calls for both more capable Data Agents and a fresh look at the infrastructure beneath them.
